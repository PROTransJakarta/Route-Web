Method & limitations

Routes are resampled into short pieces and projected to a local metric plane. Two pieces match when they run within the tolerance of each other (both directions) and their bearings differ by less than the angle – direction is ignored, so reversed routes match. All shapes of one route are merged first, so several trips of the same route count once. A shared stretch is reported once, with the set of routes that share it; stretches shorter than the minimum length are ignored.

Limitations: pure geometry matching without a road network. Parallel roads (e.g. busway beside a carriageway) closer than the tolerance may be merged; imprecise or differently digitised shapes may be missed. "Likely / Probable / Uncertain" is a heuristic from the average offset relative to the tolerance and the corridor length, not a probability – check uncertain (dashed) matches visually.

Headway & frequencies

Scheduled headway from frequencies.txt. Frequency = 3600 / headway(s).

Calculation method & assumptions

Scheduled headway is headway_secs of one frequencies.txt record over its start_time–end_time interval. Frequency = 3600 / headway. Headways are never added or multiplied.

Combined headway is only calculated where two or more active records of the same route and direction overlap in time: frequencies are summed first, then combined headway = 3600 / total frequency. Such rows are labelled Combined (calculated); single records are labelled Scheduled.

Service days: trips are limited to service_ids active on the chosen date via calendar.txt and calendar_dates.txt. Without calendar files, services are listed separately and never combined across different service_ids.

exact_times: 0/empty = frequency-based, the headway is a nominal average and vehicles do not run to an exact timetable; 1 = trips depart exactly at start_time + n×headway (< end_time). Frequencies apply to the departure of each trip from its first stop; times in stop_times.txt are only used as offsets. Nothing is derived from stop_times when frequencies.txt is missing.

Time-weighted mean frequency is Σ(frequency × duration) / Σduration; its equivalent headway is 3600 divided by that mean (not the average of headways).

GTFS validator

Checks the loaded ZIP against the GTFS Schedule reference. Your data is never modified.

What is checked?

Files: required files/columns, empty files, delimiter, malformed rows. References: route_id, service_id, trip_id, stop_id, shape_id, agency_id, parent_station. Consistency: duplicate keys, missing values, coordinates, time formats, stop_sequence, arrival/departure order, shape_dist_traveled, frequencies, calendars, colours, route types. Geometry: missing/short shapes, invalid points, large gaps, terminals far from the shape.

Rules tagged spec break the GTFS specification; best practice rules are recommendations. Reported rows are spreadsheet row numbers (header = row 1). To keep the browser responsive, each rule lists at most 1,000 occurrences; the totals are always exact.
